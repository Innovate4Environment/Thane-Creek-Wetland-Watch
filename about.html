Thane Creek Wetland Watch

Thane Creek Wetland Watch: Mapping Wetland Change and Monitoring Migratory Bird Habitats is a Social Action Project developed under the Innovate4Environment UK–India Youth Skills Partnership. The project focuses on selected wetlands within the Thane Creek landscape, an ecologically important coastal region associated with mangroves, intertidal habitats and migratory bird populations.

The project applies remote sensing and Geographic Information Systems (GIS), supported by available secondary data, to examine changes in wetland landscapes and bring together information on bird habitats and environmental pressures. By presenting these different information sources through an interactive web map, the project seeks to make environmental information more accessible and improve understanding of the relationship between wetland change and habitat conservation.

The platform covers selected sites including Panje Wetland, NRI Complex Wetland, Training Ship Chanakya (TSC) Wetland, Bhandup Pumping Station (BPS) Wetland and Airoli Wetland. It is intended to support environmental education, spatial exploration and awareness of wetland conservation challenges.

The broader aim is to demonstrate how geospatial technologies and accessible environmental information can contribute to informed discussion, further research and collaborative efforts to protect wetlands and the biodiversity they support.

Project focus

Initiative: Innovate4Environment UK–India Youth Skills Partnership
